The short version

The ad sells one product, OX.Cloud, behind an elite logo wall (IBM, Microsoft, Intel, Swisscom) and a real proof stat, 11,237 alerts reduced to 123 actionable ones. Then the click lands on a page selling 4 products and 8 capabilities, under an H1, Prompt to Run-time Security, that reads like a typo and means nothing outside the category. Specific intent, generic destination.
