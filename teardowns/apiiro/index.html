The short version

The hook is sharp and the click promise dies on impact. The ad announces three brand layers, Shift Left Is Dead, Continuous Threat Modeling, Guardian Agent, with zero numbers, zero proof, and no product shown. Then the click lands on a 1,000-word CEO blog post with the product buried in paragraph 7. The strongest capability never appears in the ad, and the Guardian Agent is named four times but never shown.
