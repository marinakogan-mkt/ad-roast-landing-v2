What the roast found · tap to open each one

What’s happening

“Control AI Actions” and “AI Agents Prove Zero Trust Doesn’t Stop at Login” land the idea fast, and the blocked and allowed paths on the creative show it.

What’s happening

“Runtime context provides continuous enforcement” sounds right and stays abstract. There is no scenario of what gets blocked or allowed.

The fix

Show one action an agent tries, and what happens to it.

What’s happening

No named customer, analyst citation or stat appears in the ad.

The fix

Bring the Gartner reference from the blog into the creative.

What’s happening

“Zero Trust for AI Agents Starts After Login” is sharp, and the piece gives a solid framework with a Gartner autonomy ladder. The reader gets what the ad promised.

What’s happening

The piece reads as pure thought leadership, with almost no tie to Cato’s product.

The fix

Add one section on how Cato enforces this, using the same argument.

What’s happening

The navigation has “Product Demo” and “Contact us,” but the body never moves the reader toward them.

The fix

End the article with a next step that continues the argument.

Why it scores 6/10 · tap a row

’Control AI Actions’ and the creative’s ’AI Agents Prove Zero Trust Doesn’t Stop at Login’ both land the core idea fast, a real CISO-relevant hook.
’Read the Blog’ matches the content offer with zero friction, correct ask for a non-gated educational piece.
Robot icon with blocked/allowed paths to folder, chat, terminal visually echoes ’authentication alone can’t govern every action’ well.
’Runtime context provides continuous enforcement’ sounds good but stays abstract, no concrete scenario of what gets blocked or allowed.
No named customer, analyst citation, or stat in the ad itself; LinkedIn allows image-based proof but this creative carries none.

Questions, answered

The point of view. The ad makes a specific claim about Zero Trust and AI agents, and the blog delivers it with a real framework.
Message match gets the reader to finish the article. It does not move them toward buying. Here nothing connects the argument to Cato’s product.
Keep the argument, then add one section that ties it to the product and one next step in the body of the article.
