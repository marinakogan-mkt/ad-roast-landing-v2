Why is Hyperproof's privacy ad converting below its potential?

H
Hyperproofhyperproof.io · August 17, 2026
CybersecurityGRC / TPRMPrivacy / DPOMessage continuity
6/10Overall funnel
The short version

The ad is built for one very specific person, a DPO or privacy lead responsible for sub-processors, personal data, and GDPR exposure, and its hook is one of the sharpest in the series. Then the landing page switches to a broad GRC story for CISOs, compliance, and internal audit. The words that made the ad work (sub-processor, personal data) disappear. The ad has focus, the page has assets, the funnel needs alignment.

A privacy click lands on a generic compliance page.

👍

What the ad gets right

  • The hook is precise, under 12 words, and gives privacy teams a concrete reason to click.
  • The page is not weak: strong structure, credible customer logos, and a genuinely low-friction form.
  • "No credit card" and "no vendor logins required" are real product advantages, repeated well.
🔥

Where it breaks after the click

  • The page drops the exact privacy language that made the ad convert.
  • The visitor gets useful messaging, but it is a different message than the one they clicked.
  • One privacy buyer is handed a broad program story instead of the specific problem they carry.

The fixes I would prioritize

1

Build a privacy-specific version of the landing page.

2

Move the "34% still on spreadsheets" stat into the hero section.

3

Add one named testimonial from a privacy or compliance leader with a measurable result.

“If I click because I care about sub-processors, I should land on a page that speaks directly to sub-processors.”
Marina Kogan, on the Hyperproof funnel
