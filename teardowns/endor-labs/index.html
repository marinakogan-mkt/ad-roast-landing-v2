The short version

One of the strongest B2B AppSec case studies this quarter, arranged in the wrong order. The Rubrik story has a named company, a named executive, and real numbers, a 24-hour SLA and 187 compromised npm packages cleared in 30 minutes. The ad body mentions none of it and turns every hard number into a soft adjective, so Tight SLAs replaces 24-hour SLA. The operator quote that would stop the scroll sits unused.
