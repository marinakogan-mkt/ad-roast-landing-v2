Why is Descope's sharp CIAM ad sending buyers to the wrong page?

D
Descopedescope.com · August 3, 2026
CybersecurityCIAM / IdentityAgentic identityAudience mismatch
Wrong pageDiagnosis
The short version

The ad copy is genuinely strong and category-aware, written for CIAM leaders with a real strategic problem around agentic identity. Then the click lands on a 3,000-word DevRel tutorial full of Python and MCP snippets. Descope clearly has two ICPs, developers and CIAM leaders, and paid traffic is being pushed into the wrong experience. You do not need more traffic when the conversation after the click falls apart this badly.

The ad to landing page journey

The ad
"Auth stops at token issuance" and "NHIs outnumber humans 20:1"
"57% of CIAM leaders fear agents sharing data": specific, category-aware
No customer logos, no Descope-specific proof points
Image CTA: "See the strategic read"
→click
The landing page
"Monitoring and Auditing Agentic Identities": Python + MCP + Snowflake snippets
Byline: Developer Relations Engineer
Zero customer logos, zero testimonials, zero named customers
3,000-word technical post

A strategic ad, an implementation tutorial.

👍

What the ad gets right

  • The ad copy is specific, category-aware, and written for a CIAM audience with a real strategic problem.
  • Every line earns attention: token issuance, non-human identities, consent after the token.
  • It meets problem-aware buyers exactly where their thinking is.
🔥

Where it breaks after the click

  • Audience: the ad speaks to CIAM leaders, the page serves developers.
  • Author signal: the ad sounds brand-level, the landing experience feels DevRel-led.
  • Content type: the ad hints at a strategic read, the page delivers an implementation tutorial.
  • Buying stage: the ad meets problem-aware buyers, the page assumes implementation-ready intent.

The fixes I would prioritize

1

Keep the developer blog for organic traffic, and send paid traffic to a true Agentic Identity Hub page.

2

Add customer logos and one named testimonial with a metric.

3

Segment campaigns by role: developer ads to the blog, CIAM ads to the product.

4

Replace the CTA with "Get the CIAM buyer's guide" or "Book a walkthrough".
