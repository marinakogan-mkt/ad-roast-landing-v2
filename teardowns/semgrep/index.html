The short version

The Snyk comparison page has the right strategy and fights its own execution. The ad leads with a screenshotable stat, 17.5 hours vs 26 minutes, plus an elite logo wall (Dropbox, Snowflake, Figma, GitLab) and a real third-party benchmark. Then the landing page says 2.5 hours, not 26 minutes, so a page selling accuracy cannot keep its own numbers straight. A $100 gift card pulls gift-card hunters, not AppSec leaders.
