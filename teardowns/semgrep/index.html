What’s happening

The ad leads with a number you could screenshot, 17.5 hours vs 26 minutes, backs it with recognizable logos (Dropbox, Snowflake, Figma, GitLab), and cites a real third-party benchmark. For a competitive-displacement play against Snyk, that is the right opening.

What’s happening

The ad says 26 minutes. The landing page says 2.5 hours. A page whose entire argument is accuracy cannot keep its own headline number straight between the ad and the destination, and a skeptical AppSec buyer will notice.

The fix

Make every number match. Pick the real figure and use it identically in the ad, the page and the benchmark, so the accuracy claim survives the click.

What’s happening

The incentive attracts gift-card hunters, not AppSec leaders. It inflates form fills with people who will never buy and buries the real buyers in the noise.

The fix

Drop the gift card, or gate it behind a qualifying step, so the page measures intent instead of rewarding tourists.
