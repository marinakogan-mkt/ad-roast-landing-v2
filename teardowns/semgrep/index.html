“AI writes your code?” stops the scroll.
“Learn how” gives it nowhere to go.

Semgrep’s webinar ad opens with the question AppSec teams are already asking. Then the promise goes soft: learn how to keep it secure, on a stock billboard, with no hint of what the session teaches. The page knows the better answer. It talks about real, reachable vulnerabilities without the noise, and that line never made it into the ad. The webinar ran on September 24 and the page now serves it on demand.

What the roast found · tap to open each one

What’s happening

“AI writes your code?” is the exact anxiety this buyer has right now. It is short, direct and needs no category knowledge.

What’s happening

“Learn how to keep it secure” could sit on any webinar. It never mentions noise, triage or reachable vulnerabilities, the things an AppSec team cares about.

The fix

Borrow the page’s own line: find reachable vulnerabilities in AI-generated code without drowning in noise.

What’s happening

A lit billboard in a dark studio has nothing to do with code, AppSec or AI. The visual adds a frame and no meaning.

The fix

Show the problem: a pull request written by an agent with one reachable finding flagged.

What’s happening

The creative gives the date and the BridgeIT partnership, then stops. There is no written next step, so the reader has to work out that this is a signup.

The fix

Put the action on the creative: save your seat, with the date beside it.

What’s happening

The title, the topic and BridgeIT all carry over, and the page names both speakers. The ad’s claim holds after the click.

What’s happening

“Book demo” and “Try for free” sit above the signup form, and the form’s own button just says “Submit.” The one action this page exists for has the weakest label.

The fix

Change “Submit” to “Watch the session” and let it be the loudest button on the page.

Why it scores 6/10 · tap a row

’AI writes your code? Learn how to keep it secure.’ is a clear question-hook that names the exact anxiety this ICP has right now.
No written CTA button text on the creative itself, just a date and partner name; viewer has to infer this is a sign-up ask.
Billboard-in-a-dark-studio visual is generic stock-concept, doesn’t connect to code, AppSec, or the AI theme the copy raises.
’Learn how to keep it secure’ is vague; no mention of false positives, triage burden, or specific tooling pain this ICP actually has.
BridgeIT co-host adds some credibility but there’s no speaker name, title, or proof point suggesting why this webinar is worth 45 minutes.

Questions, answered

The hook works and the promise behind it does not. “AI writes your code?” earns attention, then “learn how to keep it secure” gives no specific reason to register.
A webinar asks for time as well as an email. The reader needs to know what they will leave with, and a topic alone does not tell them.
They can stay in the navigation, but the signup has to be the strongest action on the page. If “Submit” is the quietest button, the page is arguing against itself.
