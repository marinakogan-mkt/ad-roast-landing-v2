What the roast found · tap to open each one

What’s happening

“From developer bottleneck to marketing-owned growth” pins the exact problem and exactly who feels it, in one line.

The fix

Keep it, this is the model other ads should copy.

What’s happening

The Greenhouse example with “launch pages 2x faster” turns the claim into evidence a buyer can trust.

The fix

Stack a second and third customer logo to deepen the proof.

What’s happening

“Learn more” doesn’t match the momentum of the headline, and on a self-serve product it quietly under-asks.

The fix

Match the CTA to the next step: “Start building” or “See it live,” not “Learn more.”

What’s happening

The page can carry the same named-customer outcome the ad used; a broad landing dilutes the specific promise that earned the click.

The fix

Lead the page with the Greenhouse-style proof and the same 2x outcome the ad opened with.

Why it scores 7/10 · tap a row

“From developer bottleneck to marketing-owned growth” names the exact pain and the exact buyer in one line. Clear and specific.
The Greenhouse example with “launch pages 2x faster” backs the growth claim with concrete, named-customer proof.
Substantive: a clear hook, proof, and a real outcome for its market.
The Greenhouse logo and name is solid proof for LinkedIn’s image format, but one example is thin, two or three logos would hit harder.
“Learn more” is generic and doesn’t match the sharp headline. On a self-serve product, a stronger action-oriented CTA would convert better.

Questions, answered
