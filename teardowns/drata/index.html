What the roast found · tap to open each one

What’s happening

A-LIGN, AWS and Drata appear as logos on the creative. For this audience that is real credibility, and it matches the promise of expert speakers in the copy.

What’s happening

“A practical framework for using ISO 42001 to support EU AI Act compliance” is on the creative. “Simplify Your AI Compliance Strategy” is the vaguer line above it.

The fix

Use the creative’s line as the ad headline too.

What’s happening

The topics are specific, but “without doubling your compliance workload” could sit on any compliance ad. There is no concrete finding to tease.

The fix

Tease one thing the webinar reveals about where the EU AI Act and ISO overlap.

What’s happening

Same speakers, same topics, same EU AI Act and ISO 42001 framing. The reader lands exactly where the ad said they would.

What’s happening

“Best Practices: Aligning EU AI Act, ISO Standards & AI Governance for Scalable Compliance” is accurate, and it buries the sharper line from the creative.

The fix

Open the page with the creative’s headline.

What’s happening

Six required fields and a 190-country dropdown is heavy for an on-demand watch, even with the right audience.

The fix

Cut the form to what sales actually uses, and let the replay do the qualifying.

Why it scores 7/10 · tap a row

’A practical framework for using ISO 42001 to support EU AI Act compliance’ on the creative is sharper than the ad copy’s vaguer ’Simplify Your AI Compliance Strategy.’
’Watch Now’ on the creative is clear and fits an on-demand asset, but the LP form has six fields plus a giant country dropdown, more friction than a webinar replay needs.
Creative headline, ’on-demand webinar’ tag and three logos (A-LIGN, AWS, Drata) match the ad body’s promise of experts covering EU AI Act and ISO overlap.
Ad lists real topics (overlap, scalable framework, high-risk prep) but ’how to keep up without doubling your compliance workload’ is a generic hook, not a concrete teaser finding.
Three named co-presenting brands (A-LIGN, AWS, Drata) shown as logos on the creative give strong credibility for a LinkedIn asset.

Questions, answered

The proof. A-LIGN, AWS and Drata together on the creative carry real weight with a GRC audience, and the page keeps the same message.
As few as sales actually uses. An on-demand replay is a low-commitment offer, so a long form makes the trade feel uneven.
Cut the form and carry the creative’s headline onto the page. The ad and the message are already working.
