Why does Cycode's analyst-proof ad lose momentum after the click?

C
Cycodecycode.com · August 24, 2026

3/10Ad to LP alignment
The short version

The ad wins the click with credible analyst proof (Gartner, IDC, GigaOm, Frost & Sullivan) aimed straight at CISO and AppSec buyers. Then the landing page walks away from that exact argument and swaps it for internal jargon like ADLC and Software Supply Chain Security. The message that earned attention gets replaced by the message the company wants to explain, and that is where the momentum dies.

The ad to landing page journey

The ad
Analyst proof up front: Gartner, IDC, GigaOm, Frost & Sullivan
"The Leader Four Analysts Agree On": awkward, and leader in what?
Names the risk, but not what Cycode specifically prevents
CTA: Request a Demo
→click
The landing page
Opens on "#1 AI Native Agentic Development Security Platform"
Analyst badges that sold the click are gone above the fold
Real logos (UBS, Broadcom, Nutanix, Unity, Elastic), but disconnected from the ad promise
Form + competing free-trial CTA

The analyst proof that earned the click disappears above the fold.

👍

What the ad gets right

  • Leads with analyst validation, which is smart for a consolidation-minded security buyer already familiar with the category.
  • The problem framing feels relevant to the CISO and AppSec audience it targets.
  • "Request a Demo" is the right CTA for that intent.
🔥

Where it breaks after the click

  • The landing page trades analyst-backed leadership for internal language (ADLC, Software Supply Chain Security).
  • The four analyst badges are missing above the fold, so the one thing that earned attention is hidden.
  • The form still asks "How did you hear about us?" even though a retargeted LinkedIn click already answers that.
  • A competing free-trial CTA splits intent instead of focusing it on the demo.

The fixes I would prioritize

1

Carry the analyst proof onto the landing page, badges above the fold.

2

Replace acronyms with plain buyer language.

3

Complete and clarify the landing-page H1 so it finishes the ad's promise.

4

Remove the competing CTA and the redundant "How did you hear about us?" field.

What does Cycode's ad get right?

Leads with analyst validation, which is smart for a consolidation-minded security buyer already familiar with the category. The problem framing feels relevant to the CISO and AppSec audience it targets. "Request a Demo" is the right CTA for that intent.
